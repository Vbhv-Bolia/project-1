Accordion

Collapsible content for FAQs and policies. One section open at a time. Keyboard accessible with arrow keys.

Applicants must have completed their higher secondary education with qualifying marks in relevant subjects. Specific requirements vary by programme and are detailed on the admissions page.

Financial aid applications are submitted alongside admission forms. Merit-based and need-based scholarships are available. Visit the Scholarships and Financial Aid section for current schemes.

Campus visits can be arranged through the Admissions Office during designated open days. Contact the office to schedule a guided tour.
