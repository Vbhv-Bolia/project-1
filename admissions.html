Programs Offered

Artificial Intelligence Chemical Engineering Civil Engineering Computer Science Electrical Engineering Materials Engineering Mechanical Engineering

Admissions Journey

1

Check Eligibility

Candidates must qualify the JEE Advanced examination and meet the Class XII performance criteria set by JAB.

2

Submit Application

Apply through the Joint Seat Allocation Authority (JoSAA) online portal during the counseling period.

3

Seat Allocation

Wait for the multi-round seat allocation process based on your rank and preferences.

4

Registration

Complete document verification and on-campus enrollment before the Foundation Programme begins.

Scholarships & Financial Aid

IIT Gandhinagar is committed to ensuring that no student is denied education due to financial constraints. Over 40% of our students receive some form of financial assistance.

Awarded to UG students based on their academic merit and parental income. Provides full or partial tuition fee waiver and a monthly stipend.

Provides opportunities for students to assist in academic and administrative tasks on campus and earn a stipend.
